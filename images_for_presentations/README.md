# Images for the presentation

Screenshots and one photo used in the pitch deck. The app screenshots are from SimForge running on our laptop with Ansys Student 2025 R2. None of these images were made with AI.

| File | What it shows | Slide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `cover.png` | Cantilever beam on the Moon, Fine mesh. Safety factor 2.52 at the support, 2.96 away from it. | Cover |
| `problem.png` | ISS common hatch in the Space Station Systems Trainer. Photo: NASA, JSC2001-02112, via [DVIDS](https://www.dvidshub.net/image/841410/iss-common-air-rack-and-common-hatch). | The problem |
| `how.png` | Confirmation card for the beam: Flat face 1 fixed, Flat face 3 loaded with 20 N down. | How it works |
| `compare-nut.png` | Hex flange nut at the Fine mesh. Safety factor 1.36 at the support, 4.15 away from it. | Comparison |
| `check.png` | Beam stress colors and the result card (93 MPa away from the support, safety factor 2.96). | Hand-calculation check and the AI slide |
| `bracket_moon.png` | L bracket with a 30 kg load on the Moon. Safety factor 3.19. | Who it helps |
| `bracket_mars.png` | The same bracket and load on Mars. Safety factor 1.38, below the 1.4 target. | Who it helps |

The two bracket runs use the same mesh (35,907 elements). Peak stress is 87 MPa on the Moon and 199 MPa on Mars, about 2.3 times higher, which matches the ratio of the two surface gravities.
